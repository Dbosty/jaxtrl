Before presenting results, we summarize our experimental configuration.
All experiments use the $25\times25$ pursuit-evasion environment described
in Section~V, with episodes capped at 100 timesteps and discount factor
$\gamma=0.99$. We construct the bounded-rational policy library with
$K{=}8$ using Algorithm~1: each level is obtained by perturbing the
previous one within an $L_2$ ball of radius
$\eta_{\text{pursuer}}=\eta_{\text{evader}}=0.05$ before training against
it, the level-0 evader mixture is taken over $N{=}10$ weights between
$\pi_{A^*}$ and $\pi_{\text{random}}$, and each of the resulting 17
best-response stages (9 pursuer, 8 evader) is trained independently for
$2.0\times10^7$ environment steps with PPO. PAP, RLBO's meta-controller,
and both CBOs are then trained under a matched budget of
$4.0\times10^7$ environment steps, collected in batches of 250 parallel
environments over 100 steps per iteration. All level-$k$, PAP, and RLBO
policies share an LSTM actor-critic with hidden size 64 and a $[64,32,16]$
ReLU head, optimized with Adam ($\text{lr}=3\times10^{-4}$) under standard
PPO hyperparameters ($\lambda_{\text{GAE}}=0.95$, clip $\epsilon=0.2$,
entropy coefficient $5\times10^{-3}$, value coefficient $0.5$, 10 epochs
per update, sub-batch size $50{,}000$); RLBO's meta-controller differs only
in using a categorical head over the $K{+}1{=}9$ pretrained pursuer levels
in place of PAP's continuous action space. CBO-On's classifier shares the
same $[64,32,16]$ ReLU architecture and is trained via DAgger over a FIFO
buffer of $200{,}000$ labeled transitions, with 5 supervised epochs per
rollout batch, to the same $4.0\times10^7$-step budget; CBO-Off instead
trains once over a fixed dataset collected from all $(K{+}1)\times K$
policy pairs (500 environments $\times$ 100 steps each), for 50 epochs.
Unless otherwise noted, all reported figures evaluate 500 environments per
rollout, and error bars denote $\pm1$ standard error of the mean over
episodes.
